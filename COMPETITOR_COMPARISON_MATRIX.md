# Competitor Comparison Matrix

**Research date:** 2026-11-21 · **Sites:** 16 · **Prices shown in each site's own currency as displayed**

Legend: **Verified** = stated on a page fetched · **Observed** = visible but unconfirmed · **Not disclosed** = no public source found · **Needs verification** = requires company confirmation.

> **Currency note.** Only the three Malaysian sites display MYR natively, so only
> they are true price benchmarks. International prices are recorded in their own
> currency exactly as displayed. **No conversions are applied anywhere in this
> document** — a converted figure carried into our catalogue would be an invented
> price, and our rule is that the owner sets every price. Any future conversion
> must use a named rate, be labelled approximate, and never be mistaken for a
> selling price.

> **Honest note on depth.** The eight pet sites were not audited to equal depth.
> Mackenzie & George and Dogs & Horses were read closely — sizing, lead times,
> materials, guarantees — because they publish the detail that matters. The
> remaining five were reviewed for positioning, range and any publishable
> sizing or material information, and where none was found the row says so
> rather than being filled with inference. **A comparison matrix that pretends
> to equal depth across unequal evidence is worse than one that admits the gap.**

## 4. Cross-cutting comparison — where we stand

| Dimension | Best competitor practice | Our position | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shipping transparency | BONIA: free nationwide + explicit windows per region | We state cost is confirmed before dispatch; no invented rates | **Correct as-is** until courier accounts exist |
| Courier transparency | Braun Büffel: names GDEX | We name no courier | Correct as-is; name one when appointed |
| Delivery estimates | BONIA: 2–4 / 2–5 / 3–5 / 3–6 by region | We give an honest window and confirm on WhatsApp | Acceptable; refine when measured |
| Payment depth | BONIA: 7 methods incl. FPX, e-wallets, BNPL | **COD only** | Deliberate. Phase D only |
| Sizing help (pet) | M&G: dual-unit chart with recommended fit | **Was absent — now added** | **Done this phase** |
| Warranty | Bellroy: tiered, per-category, published | Not published | Owner decision; do not invent |
| Returns | M&G: 90 days free | Policy exists; window is owner's | Owner decision |
| Product page depth | Bellroy: capacity-first specification | Dimensions present; capacity not led | Roadmap, essential |
| Gift bundling | Leather Avenue: wallet + belt set | No bundles | Roadmap, optional (owner prices it) |
| Trust signals | BONIA: reviews, order tracking, care guides | Contact channels + policies | Roadmap, essential |
| Localisation | Mulberry: regional storefronts | **EN / MS / ZH, full** | Already ahead of all three MY sites |
| Broken pages | Leather Avenue has 3 dead info pages | Build fails on dead internal links | Already ahead |

**The honest summary.** We cannot beat BONIA on breadth, delivery speed or
payment options, and we should not try. We are already ahead on trilingual
content, on telling the truth about delivery instead of implying a promise, and —
as of this phase — on pet sizing guidance. The categories where a small
manufacturer-direct brand can genuinely win are **product specificity,
material honesty, and fit guidance**. That is the strategy the roadmap follows.
